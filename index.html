HS VANCE · OPENVANCE PROJECT · ANDROID

Video, on your terms.

HS Vance, developed as the OpenVance project, is an independent Android video application for discovering and watching YouTube content. It includes search, playback controls, background audio and picture-in-picture.

Connecting your Google account

Connecting an account is optional. With your permission, OpenVance uses YouTube data to show your channel and supported library items such as liked videos, playlists and subscriptions. When you choose, it can submit a video rating or comment to YouTube.
