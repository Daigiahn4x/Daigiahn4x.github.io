Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

OpenVance is an independent Android application. Connecting a Google account is optional.

Google and YouTube data

When you authorize Google access, OpenVance uses YouTube Data API services to read your YouTube channel details, liked videos, playlists, subscriptions and video ratings, and to display them in the app. When you request it, OpenVance can send ratings, comments and replies to YouTube. The Google authorization screen describes the permissions requested, including the broader access provided by the YouTube force-ssl scope. The app also requests your email address for account identification and authorization renewal.

OpenVance uses this data to provide its visible account, library and interaction features. These requests are sent to Google and YouTube to perform the functions you choose. Google's use of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage access at Google Account permissions.

Storage and other services

Account profile details and library information are stored in app data on your device; the current OAuth access token is held in memory. Your device's Android backup settings may back up app data. OpenVance also stores local viewing history, watch-later items and settings. Searches, video identifiers and requests for content may be sent to YouTube, Google, image hosts and third-party Piped API services. These services receive network information such as your IP address and apply their own privacy policies.

Disconnecting and deleting local data

Signing out asks the Google authorization provider to revoke access and clears account preferences; revocation can also be managed in your Google Account. Library data may remain on the device after signing out. You can remove local app data using Android settings or by uninstalling the app; device backups, downloaded files and data held by other services may require separate removal.
